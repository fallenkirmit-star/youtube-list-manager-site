Privacy Policy — YouTube List Manager

Last updated: October 31, 2026

What this app is

YouTube List Manager is a private, single-user application. It is operated by one individual, for that individual's own YouTube account only. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's own Google sign-in and consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API v3 (scope: youtube.force-ssl) to access the owner's own YouTube account. In practice it reads:

  • The list of channels the owner follows, and those channels' recent uploads (titles, video IDs, publish dates).
  • The contents of the owner's own playlists, to avoid adding a video twice.

It adds videos to the owner's own playlists. It does not post, comment, or change anything else on the account.

How the data is used

The data is used for one purpose only: noticing new uploads from channels the owner follows and adding them to the owner's own playlists. Run logs and lists of video IDs are kept on the owner's own computer so the app does not add duplicates.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The only services involved are Google's own YouTube APIs, under Google's terms.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke this app's access at any time from Google Account permissions.
